Editorial approach:
- Historical and religious narratives identified as tradition or belief are presented as such, not as independently established fact.
- Festival dates follow annual calendars and may shift; no fixed date is presented as a permanent annual date.
- Ferry services, lighthouse access, museum schedules, prices and other operational details are not treated as permanent facts.
- Distances shown by the map and planner are based on approximate point coordinates; straight-line distances are not road distances.
- Safety guidance remains where it materially affects coastal, rocky-shore, river-mouth or seasonal travel.
