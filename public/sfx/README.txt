Sound effects used by the site. Events and files are listed in lib/sfx.js (the SFX object); volumes are in the VOL object there.
whoosh-transition.mp3 and transition-soft.mp3 and logo-reveal.mp3 are trimmed copies (their originals had long silent tails).
Browsers only allow audio after the first click or key press, so the first sound plays after that. Reduced-motion visitors get no sound.
